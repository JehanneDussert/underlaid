## Underlaid v0.4.0 — emergency departments by time of day, status of the tests, accessibility fixes, retired indicators

**Draft.** Date to be set when the release is created. This release brings together PRs #21, #23, #29 and #30.

### Emergency departments by departure time (#23)

The neighbourhood page now also gives the time to the nearest emergency department in the evening (Tuesday 21:00) and on Sunday (10:00), next to the night (1:00), on foot and by public transport. These rows are information only: they are not counted in "the nearest" nor in the position of the need. The Method page has a new section with a table:

| Departure | Without constraint | Wheelchair | More than 45 min in a wheelchair (neighbourhoods) | (residents) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tuesday 10:00 | 18 min | 27 min | 6.4 % | 6.7 % |
| Tuesday 21:00 | 19 min | 27 min | 7.5 % | 7.8 % |
| Sunday 10:00 | 19 min | 28 min | 8.2 % | 8.7 % |
| Night (1:00) | 23 min | 38 min | 37.6 % | 39.0 % |

Medians over the 2,690 inhabited neighbourhoods; a neighbourhood not reached within 90 minutes counts as more than 45 minutes. At night, accessibility is not given for about half of the Noctilien trips (191 of 372 between 1:00 and 2:00); counting them as accessible, the share beyond 45 minutes in a wheelchair goes from 37.6 % to 35.2 %. Every figure is produced by `scripts/analysis/emergency_time_slots.py`.

### Status of the tests against residents' means (#29)

Earlier versions called three tests "pre-registered". The repository history does not support it: each rule was first written in unversioned working notes, and only a rule recorded in the repository before the result can count as verifiably pre-registered.

| Test | First dated record of the rule in the repository | First result | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Access to care | 2 October 2026, 11:58 (commits `0414353`, `772b475`) | 2 October 2026, 9:31 (result file, not versioned) | Not verifiably pre-registered: the rule entered the repository after the result |
| Inclusive mobility (wheelchair) | Same | Same | Same |
| Public services | 3 October 2026, 13:00 (commit `bed14ba`) | Time not verifiable; first dated record: 3 October 2026, 18:57 (commit `8f1d2c6`) | Rule recorded before the first record of the result; the time of the first calculation cannot be verified |
| Households without a car (additional test) | 3 October 2026, 22:53 (commit `08f623d`) | 3 October 2026, 22:52 (result file, not versioned) | Not verifiably pre-registered |

Paris time. The verdicts do not change. The table is on the Method page, with a dated entry on the Corrections page; the README, SCORING.md, docs/LESSONS.md and the v0.3.0 notes say the same. The sentence on the second travel-time calculation is also made precise: the rules of 5 October (bridges, terrain-model errors) were set before the calculation of the slope network and of the wheelchair routes, the first phase of the calculation (without constraint, slow walking) having started 21 minutes earlier.

### Accessibility fixes (#21, closes #18)

- **No more fading text.** Animated text on the site (home title, introduction, address form, "all everyday places" link, labels and durations on the network plan, accordion panels) now moves into place instead of fading in: while text fades, its contrast is below the WCAG 1.4.3 minimum. When leaving the home page, only the drawing fades; the labels disappear at once. Decorative animations without text (pulses, loading dots) are unchanged. With reduced motion, every animation is still instant, and the zoom when leaving the home page is skipped.
- **Language link.** The language switch shows "EN" or "FR", and its accessible name now starts with that visible text ("EN, English version", "FR, version française"), as WCAG 2.5.3 (Label in Name) requires: people who control their computer by voice can activate it by saying what they see.
- Checks: Lighthouse accessibility 100 on `/` and `/en` on desktop (three runs each) and on phone; automated accessibility checks (axe) with no serious or critical violation.

### Retired indicators removed from the published data (#30)

The downloadable neighbourhood file (`vulnerability_score_iris.geojson`) no longer contains eight columns from four indicators that the project abandoned. None of them is part of the score, and the site no longer shows any of them:

| Columns | Indicator | Why it was abandoned |
|---|---|---|
| `access_minutes_domain_C`, `access_minutes_domain_D`, `access_minutes_domain_E` | Time to the nearest equipment (INSEE 200 m grid) | Crushed against its floor: it did not tell neighbourhoods apart. Replaced in v0.2.0 by access to care weighted by capacity (E2SFCA). |
| `social_index_schools`, `social_index_middle_schools` | Social position index (IPS) of the schools in the neighbourhood | It follows residents' income by construction, which the project measures on a separate axis. |
| `pct_pmr_accessible`, `erp_count` | Acceslibre, accessibility of building entrances | Coverage too uneven between départements: a missing entry means missing information, not an inaccessible place. |
| `footway_density_m_per_km2` | Density of footways mapped in OpenStreetMap | It measures how pavements are drawn in OpenStreetMap, which differs strongly between départements, not the pavements themselves. |

**These columns remain available in v0.3.0** on Zenodo: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23401610. Every other column and value of the file is unchanged (checked: the new file equals the v0.3.0 file minus these columns).

Scripts `07_equipment_access_200m.py`, `09_social_index_schools.py`, `12_accessibility_erp.py` and `15_pedestrian_paths.py`, their intermediate files and their configuration are removed; the quarterly update no longer downloads these sources.

**Sources no longer used**: INSEE "Données sur la localisation et l'accès de la population aux équipements" (access times by grid cell); Ministry of Education "Indices de position sociale des écoles", "Indices de position sociale des collèges" and "Adresse et géolocalisation des établissements d'enseignement des premier et second degrés"; Acceslibre; the OpenStreetMap Overpass API. The travel times to schools, collèges and lycées are not affected: they use the INSEE permanent equipment database (BPE 2025). The OpenStreetMap street network is still used for travel times.
